Need a Different Time?

Optional scheduling additions

Depending on availability, evenings or weekends may be available for an additional $15; same-day priority requests may carry an additional $20. Any extra fee will be explained and agreed upon before your appointment.

Before we meet: Bring the complete document and acceptable identification. Do not sign in advance if your document requires signing before the notary. Notaries cannot provide legal advice or choose a notarial act for you. Remote online notarization is not currently advertised as available.
