Hi, I'm Katie!

I'm a Florida girl through and through, born and raised right here in Brevard County. I'm a mom, a beach lover, a music enthusiast, and someone who's always happiest meeting new people, exploring somewhere new, or spending time outdoors.

After having the opportunity to officiate a wedding, I realized how much I enjoyed being part of such a meaningful moment. That's what led me to begin offering wedding ceremonies through Coastal Sign & Seal.

I believe a wedding should feel like the people getting married. Whether you're dreaming of a traditional ceremony, something intimate and barefoot on the beach, a celebration that honors your faith or culture, or something entirely your own, I'd love to hear your story.

I proudly welcome LGBTQIA+ couples, including transgender and nonbinary partners, and couples of every race, religion and background. What matters to me is that you feel comfortable, respected and celebrated.

I'm based in Brevard County, but I'm always happy to travel throughout Florida and beyond.
