Wedding Officiant

From a simple exchange of vows to a celebration written around your story, there's room here for the kind of wedding that feels right to you.

A little more than “I do”

Traditional, personalized, intimate, barefoot on the beach, or somewhere completely unexpected. All couples are welcome, including LGBTQIA+ couples and couples of every background, faith and tradition.

The Simple Signing

$30

A brief, no-frills legal marriage solemnization for couples who don't want a formal ceremony.

  • Brief legal solemnization of your marriage
  • Completing the officiant portion of the marriage license as required
  • No personalized script, organized vow exchange or rehearsal

The Coastal Elopement

Starting at $175

A meaningful, short ceremony for the couple, with or without a few guests.

  • Pre-ceremony conversation about your preferences
  • A short prepared ceremony and space for personal vows
  • Light personalization of the ceremony wording
  • Legal solemnization included within the package

The Intimate Ceremony

Starting at $275

A thoughtfully organized ceremony for couples celebrating with a small gathering of loved ones.

  • Planning conversation before the wedding
  • A personalized ceremony outline with selected wording
  • Placement of one reading or special moment in the ceremony
  • Legal solemnization included within the package

The Signature Ceremony

Starting at $350

A fully written ceremony for couples who want a traditional format, modern approach, or a blend of both.

  • Detailed planning consultation
  • Custom-written ceremony script
  • Incorporation of selected traditions or readings

The Bespoke Ceremony

Starting at $450

Our most collaborative option, with a more detailed story-led ceremony and additional script development.

Pricing under review: These are proposed travel fees, not yet final published rates. Travel times are approximate and measured one way from the general Palm Bay service area to the venue, not from a private residence. Overnight accommodations, extraordinary parking or venue expenses, and separate rehearsal trips are quoted and approved before booking. The exact venue, date, and route will be confirmed before a reservation is accepted. Legal authority to officiate outside Florida must be verified for each destination.

Good to know: These are proposed package inclusions and starting prices, subject to final confirmation. The statutory marriage solemnization portion is $30; higher package prices include additional planning, writing and coordination services. Travel beyond the agreed local area, rehearsals and other extras are quoted separately before booking. Florida and out-of-state travel may be available by arrangement.
