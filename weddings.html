Good to know: Package inclusions and starting prices are proposed and subject to confirmation. For Florida notary-officiated weddings, up to $30 covers the statutory solemnization; amounts above that cover separately described optional non-notarial services such as planning and script writing. Couples provide a valid Florida marriage license. Rehearsals, additional planning, special equipment, venue fees and other extras are not included unless specifically agreed in writing. Travel charges, if applicable, are confirmed before booking. Florida and out-of-state travel may be available by arrangement.
